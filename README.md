# LePontCy — Bridges of Solidarity Hackathon 2020

## Overview
**LePontCy** was developed during the **Bridges of Solidarity Hackathon (2020)** in response to the **Beirut explosion** that occurred in August 2020.  
The platform was created to connect people in Beirut with individuals and organizations around the world willing to help by donating goods, services, or funds.

---

## Purpose
After the devastating Beirut explosion, thousands of residents were left without homes, supplies, or essential support.  
LePontCy (“The Bridge of Cyprus”) was designed to serve as a **digital bridge** between those in need and those able to assist, emphasizing empathy, solidarity, and collective action.

---

## Features
- **Needs Listing:** Residents could post what they urgently required (food, housing, supplies, repairs).  
- **Global Donations:** Donors worldwide could browse verified needs and contribute directly.  
- **Cause Management:** Included sections for causes, donation tracking, and event listings.  
- **Authentication System:** Users could register, log in, and manage their profiles securely.  
- **Modern Frontend:** Clean and responsive UI using SCSS, JS, and Bootstrap-like design components.

## Project Structure
Key directories and files:
- `index.php` — homepage  
- `causes.php`, `causeDetails.php` — cause listings and details  
- `donation.php`, `checkout.php` — donation processing pages  
- `auth.php`, `my-account.php`, `viewProfile.php` — authentication and user management  
- `dashboard/` — admin and internal management pages  
- `css/`, `js/`, `scss/`, `vendors/`, `images/` — static assets  
- `footer.php`, `navbar.php` — shared layout components  

---

## Impact
LePontCy demonstrated how technology can be used to **mobilize compassion and practical support** in times of crisis.  
By enabling direct communication between those affected and those able to help, the project showcased the potential of tech-driven humanitarian action.
